✦ The Process

Recover Dues in 3 Simple Steps

From invoice upload to payment — RecoverAI automates every touchpoint in your recovery pipeline.

1
Upload Your Invoices
Import unpaid invoices from CSV, or sync directly from Zoho Books & Tally. RecoverAI auto-classifies every invoice by overdue days, risk level, and next action.
2
AI Sends Smart Reminders
Gemini AI crafts personalized WhatsApp messages in Hindi or English. The AI reads each reply, classifies intent, and auto-escalates from polite reminder → legal notice.
3
Get Paid. Mark Recovered.
Once payment lands, mark it recovered in one click. Analytics update instantly. Export to Tally or Zoho, and generate a compliance report for your records.
